# The Valley That Refuses to Keep It

## I. Where T002 Stood

*The newborn thing had already been told what it was, before anyone asked what it would do. It was starved on five sides and fed on one. It faced the world backwards, as instructed. It belonged to some larger country it could not yet see the shape of, and it wanted more than it had been given. All of this was true of it and none of it had touched it yet — knowledge only, waiting to become weather.*

Cartography left T002 as an Edge-Starved, Reversed, Region-Tangled, Hungry tile — a buried threshold, one that opens passage through layer and cost rather than a clean surface, carrying the sleeping floral girl and the burden-carrying giant as inherited pressure. None of that had been turned into an actual instruction for material yet. That's Attunement's job: take a tile's standing and turn it into pressure specific enough that Surface can finally pick up something and cut it.

## II. Four Slots, One Field

*A single hunger was given a shape to hunger in: not a scatter of wanting, but a field, a claim, a country drawn around the newborn thing whether it agreed to be drawn or not. A covering was chosen for it, soft enough to fold, thin enough to lie in more than one layer without asking permission. A borrowed logic was set beside it — not its own, but useful, the way a stranger's map can still get you home. And a question was left inside it on purpose, the one thing in the whole reading that was not meant to be answered yet.*

Echo, Green 4, gave **Claim**: choose a system — a region, route, grid, or layer logic — that may claim the tile. The result here reads as a pressure-field rather than a shape: the five starved edges pull inward toward the center, and the center refuses to hold what arrives, pushing it back out toward the SW edge, where T002 touches T001.

Matter, Blue 3, gave **Family**: pick one material family. The best fit is layered paper — collage, thin coverings, tissue — because the tile needs to bury something without deciding yet what that something is.

Mirror, Red 3, gave **Structure**: find a real-world reference with its own organizing logic. The brief calls specifically for something with directional flow — a drainage diagram, a pressure map, a radial city plan, a root system, a contour map, a route network. Train-map linework is the current front-runner, though it isn't locked in yet.

Omen, Yellow 3, gave **Question**: leave one unfinished part of the tile standing as an open question rather than an answer. Here it's this: what is the thing being buried by the inward-starved edges, and why does the center push it back toward T001 instead of keeping it?

## III. A Wandering Pressure, Turned

*Something in it had already been loose before the reading began — disconnected, misplaced, without a place to belong to. It caught, as loose things do, on the first thread it found, and having caught, it could not simply stay caught. It had to be bent. It had to be aimed. And once it was aimed, something small and nameless had to be prepared and hidden away, to stand in for the thing that still had no shape of its own.*

Pressure, Brown 6, went to the matrix instead of giving a direct read, the same way it did in Cartography. This time it broke into Green 4, **Drift** — something disconnected, wandering, or lacking relation; Blue 3, **Thread** — the pressure sits along a line, route, or repeated path; and Red 3, **Turn** — that pressure has to be redirected, rotated, or aimed elsewhere. Combined, a wandering pressure catches on a thread and has to be turned toward the SW edge and T001.

Provision, Black 4, gave **Token**: prepare one pending object or marker for the unresolved effect — something that stands in for "the buried thing" until Surface or Inscription can locate it properly.

## IV. Turning the Field Into a Valley

*And the thing that would carry all of this, once a hand finally reached for it, would not look like an instruction at all. It would look like ground giving way — a place where height and depth stop agreeing with each other, where the eye is asked to read down even as the material stands up. A road would arrive from one direction only, and it would not be allowed to arrive cleanly.*

The physical read on the Claim result is a sinkhole, or a valley — one system doing the work of Claim, Hungry, Buried Threshold, Burial Cost, and Edge-Starved all at once, which is a rare amount of agreement out of five separate results. The plan is thin paper or tissue in torn rings and crescents, built up highest along the five starved edges and thinning toward the center — but with the color going darker or more densely stained toward the center, so the eye reads the middle as deeper even though the material is physically lower there. Higher at the rim, darker at the middle: the contradiction is doing the work.

The SW edge, the one that actually touches T001, stays different on purpose — less stacked, more open, closer to a route than a rim. A fragment of train-map linework can enter from that edge and aim toward the center, but it isn't allowed to arrive as a clean, legible road. It has to look misread, half-buried, or dragged through the tissue, bending or breaking before it gets where it was going — which fits the Turn result better than a road that actually completes its trip. The five starved edges are candidates for a stranger, non-cartographic material: felt as a muffled, matted "dead rim," or gauze, a dryer sheet, torn masking tape, a used envelope window, tea-bag paper, sandpaper, or thin fabric interfacing — something that reads as thirsty and underfed rather than finished ground.

## V. What Sleeps Under the Valley

*Two things had been carried into the newborn thing before it had a shape to carry them in: one too heavy to keep standing, one too tired to stay awake. The heavy thing wanted only to be lowered somewhere it could stop being looked at. The tired thing wanted only to keep sleeping, and didn't much care what it slept on top of. Between the two of them, the valley finally had something to be a valley about.*

The buried thing gets pulled straight from the two Awakening cards rather than invented new. The giant reads as the force of carrying and burden; the colosseum reads as the thing too heavy, too historical, too public and structured to stay exposed. So the buried element becomes arena logic rather than a literal drawing of a colosseum: a ring or oval fragment, broken seating bands, arches reduced to small repeated cuts, a ruined oval partly visible under the tissue layers. The sleeping floral girl supplies the softer counter-omen for the center itself — not a literal figure, but a stump-ring or root-darkness logic, nature sleeping over architecture rather than confronting it.

The current strongest idea for the token is a small oval or ring scrap — possibly cut from the train-map linework, or a printed architecture texture — hidden under the tissue near the center but nudged slightly toward the SW edge, as if the center is actively ejecting it rather than simply failing to hold it.

## VI. What Surface Now Owes

*The court is finished with its asking, and the newborn valley goes back into the dark still hungry, still leaning all its weight toward the one edge that first let it exist. Under it, something heavy waits to be forced back out the way it came. Over it, something small and half-remembered has agreed to keep sleeping regardless of what it's sleeping on.*

So Surface inherits a specific, physical list: mark the SW edge as the release channel and keep it more open and route-like than the rest; build the other five edges up higher with torn tissue while keeping the color denser and darker toward the center, so height and depth read as opposites on purpose; bring in one structural reference with real directional flow, most likely train-map linework, and let it enter only from SW before it bends, breaks, or gets pushed back; choose a strange, non-cartographic material — felt, gauze, sandpaper, or similar — for the five starved edges; and prepare, without placing permanently yet, a small buried token standing in for a ruined arena being ejected toward T001.

What doesn't get resolved here, on purpose: the exact material for the reference structure, the final form of the buried token, and the question Attunement leaves open for Inscription — what is being buried, and why does the center refuse to hold it. Surface gets to build the valley. It doesn't get to answer that yet.
