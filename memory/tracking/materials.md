## Refinement Ideas

Other things already-owned materials could do, beyond how they've been used so far:

- Clear coat (currently glue-only on T004): could double as a light protective glaze over finished Surface/Inscription work elsewhere, not just as a no-seal adhesive for deliberately-fraying fabric.
- Splash-shaped template (currently a one-off for the T005→T004 scab): a reusable stencil for any future "spreading contamination/bleed" Inscription result, not a single-use prop.
- Leftover playing/tarot cards (stacked as T005's wall mounds): the embedded-object-under-cover technique first used with T002's plastic ring could extend to other small found objects, not just cards.
- Modeling/texture paste: only used tinted red/brown so far; untinted or differently-tinted batches could serve other Surface "weighted/accumulated" results without implying the same scab material or lore.
- Alchemy schematics and old letters/postal documents (both sepia): share an archival, technical-but-aged tone that could suit an Inheritance or Omen result calling for something old, coded, or half-legible — in the same register as T001's own electrical-diagram-scrap vocabulary, without needing to be that vocabulary.
- Fantasy map coloring book: literal map line-art is a natural source for a hidden-line/route Inheritance result (like T003's Layer + Line + Answer) or any Inscription Form asking for a route, border, or diagram mark.
- Wood/stone/sand pattern paper: a direct fit for Surface Ground results like Weighted or Rough, or Surface Substance results calling for a natural/found texture.
- Picture-and-text books (wide subject range): a flexible cut-source for Omen or Mirror results that need one specific image or text fragment rather than a whole sheet of material — pull a single element rather than committing the source book to one tile.
- Hexagon templates (inch increments): T004's large hexagon line was drawn freehand to tie its mark to "the shape of the map itself" (Inscription Relation: System). The template could make that move precise and repeatable across future tiles, or a deliberately imprecise freehand line could become a meaningful choice in its own right once precision is available and skipped on purpose.
- Architect's compass / circle & arc rulers: T002's dashed red circle and solid black circle were drawn freehand. A future tile could use the compass for a clean, intentional circle where precision itself is part of the reading (a controlled boundary) versus freehand for something meant to feel found or unstable.
- Coffee-stain paste template: a literal fit for any Surface Treatment result like T001's own Stained (currently achieved with yellow watercolor streaks) — gives a stain effect an actual stain-shaped texture instead of a painted approximation.
- Gesso: could serve as a neutral primer base for a Surface Ground result like Bare or Closed, giving a genuinely blank foundation before anything else goes on, rather than working directly on raw tile material.
- CA glue vs. PVA glue: worth choosing deliberately rather than defaulting to one — CA suits instant-bond found-object mounting (like T001's epoxy-glued fan-brush), PVA suits general paper/fabric work where some working time is useful.
