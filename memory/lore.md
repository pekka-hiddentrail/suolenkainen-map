# Suolenkainen's Map Lore

General lore, recurring voices, cosmology, rumors, and setting-level material for Suolenkainen's Map.

This file is for durable lore that is broader than a single tile. Tile-specific facts still belong in `tile-data.md`; phase-by-phase session history belongs in `suolenkainen-map.md`; unresolved practical debts belong in `open-obligations.md`.

## Higher Forces

### The Court

Status: active general lore; details still developing.

The Court is a higher force governing the map. It stands above individual tiles and sessions as an ordering, judging, witnessing, or narrating presence. Its exact nature, members, laws, motives, and limits are not fully defined yet.

Current reading:

- The Court may frame how map events are witnessed, judged, archived, or spoken about.
- The Court should not override the six-phase procedure unless a future rule explicitly says so.
- The Court can be used as lore voice, framing device, or mythic authority in website and blog writing.
- Details about The Court should be added gradually as sessions reveal them.

### The Court's Arena

Status: active T002 lore; details still developing.

T002 carries a relic of the Court: the sinkhole is an ancient colosseum or arena associated with the Court. It was always expected to emerge from the void as such, but not as a newly made surface feature. The arena existed only in the void before T002 emerged, and the tile reveals it as a relic rather than inventing it in the present.

Current reading:

- The sinkhole may be read as the exposed or collapsed presence of the Court's arena.
- The arena should remain partly relic-like: buried, ancient, and void-originating.
- Circles and ovals connected to C089 may become early signs of the arena before any literal colosseum appears.
- Later Latin text about spectators of gladiator matches may belong to this arena lore if Surface or Inscription makes room for it.

## Voices

### The Storyteller

Status: active narrator concept; provisional until further defined.

The Storyteller is the narrator of The Court. He is the voice that speaks in blog posts and public-facing lore writing. He is connected to The Court, but his exact role, personality, authority, reliability, and relationship to the map are still open.

Current use:

- Blog posts may be written as if spoken by the Storyteller.
- The Storyteller may interpret map events, but should not erase the practical session record.
- The Storyteller's voice can be more literary, courtly, mythic, or indirect than the session log.
- Future sessions may define his tone, titles, limits, motives, and whether he is reliable.
