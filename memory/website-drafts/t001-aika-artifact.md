Summary:

T001 - AIKA is the first origin tile of Suolenkainen's Map. It began from the first-tile exception: the cards aimed elsewhere, but with no existing map the work had to break the void at [0,0].

AIKA, the Finnish word for time, sits near the center in a technical font. It was partly buried under schematic scraps, then clarified with black and orange marker until it refused to disappear. A real fan-brush is fixed into the tile and partly covered with paper, leaving yellow watercolor streaks as if the brush has painted across the surface.

The north and northeast edges are marked as spread points with black/yellow warning checkers. Some black bleeds inward, but a safety line at the north/northeast corner stops the checker spread. Archaic symbols sit below and between AIKA and the safety line, giving the technical surface an older, stranger voice.

## Before / After

Before:

The map had no tile and no occupied coordinate. S001 began with two cards: C171, a robot ghost, and C111, a lion. The calculated movement did not become a placed coordinate because the first-tile exception created T001 at the origin.

After:

The map has one origin tile at [0,0]. T001 is a small heavy collage object with technical scraps, AIKA, a physical fan-brush, yellow lion-hue streaks, warning-checker spread points, checker bleed, a safety line, and archaic symbols.

## Session Diary Note

The first session turned absence into a physical object. The robot ghost became trace and technical afterimage; the lion became yellow/orange hue and force. The tile grew heavy quickly, so Inscription stayed small and precise: clarify AIKA, show the checker bleed, stop the spread with a safety line, and leave the north/northeast edges as future questions.
