# Prompt Interpretation Notes

Practical notes for reading Suolenkainen's Map prompts. This file records habits and preferred practice for applying the rules at the table, plus session-specific interpretive notes below. It does not repeat the individual keyword glosses — those live in `map-creation-rules.md`'s Reading Glosses section (Attunement Table Keywords, Matrix Reading Keywords) and should stay generic there. This file only adds what's specific to how a given session actually used one.

## General Reading Practice

- Treat each keyword as a task, not only a mood. It should eventually ask for a physical choice, a note, a restriction, a material, a mark, or a future obligation.
- If a keyword points to something that does not exist yet, record the absence instead of inventing false history. The absence can become a wound, omission, debt, or first-tile exception.
- Prefer the smallest concrete action that satisfies the prompt. A reserved scrap, a named blank, a marked edge, or a note in the log is often enough.
- If a prompt feels symbolic, ask what object, edge, layer, mark, material, or record could carry that symbol.
- A prompt can be answered later. Attunement often creates pressure; Surface and Inscription often give that pressure a body.
- When uncertain, write the uncertainty down as a live question. Do not smooth it away.

## Phase Keywords

### Awakening

- Awakening chooses the target and opens the session. It should not decide the tile's final look.
- Coordinate results are procedural first and poetic second. Calculate, adjust, and log before interpreting.
- If a rule cannot operate because the map lacks the required state, record the exception as a rules delta or Chronicle debt.

### Attunement

- Attunement creates binding pressures. It prepares the conditions that later phases must answer.
- The six Attunement results should become a short story and a concrete todo list.
- Do not force all six results into immediate mark-making. Some results only prepare, restrict, reserve, or obligate.

## Creating Attunement Outputs

### Story of Attunement

- Write exactly six sentences unless the user asks for a different length.
- Keep the sentence order fixed: Echo, Matter, Mirror, Omen, Pressure, Provision.
- Each sentence should carry one prompt result forward into later work.
- The story should be evocative, but each sentence must imply something physical or recordable.
- If a result is omitted, impossible, hidden, or unresolved, give it a sentence as an absence, debt, mystery, or pending condition.
- Do not solve Surface or Inscription inside the story. The story binds later phases; it does not finish the tile.
- Prefer active pressure words: waits, claims, withholds, divides, echoes, conceals, interrupts, anchors, asks.
- Avoid vague mood-only sentences. A good sentence points toward an edge, material, card, wound, direction, token, mark, layer, or future action.

Example shape:

1. Echo sentence: what returns, continues, fails to continue, or presses from before.
2. Matter sentence: what material is chosen, forbidden, reserved, acquired, or made available.
3. Mirror sentence: what reference, old image, card, structure, or phrase the tile must echo or resist.
4. Omen sentence: what sign, hue, question, wound, weather, or contradiction becomes binding.
5. Pressure sentence: what direction, conflict, avoidance, imbalance, or correction steers later work.
6. Provision sentence: what token, guide, mark, fragment, support, or seed is prepared off-tile.

### Attunement Todo List

- Convert the story into physical next actions before map-work begins.
- Include only actions that can be done now or deliberately held for later.
- Prefer verbs like choose, inspect, reserve, prepare, label, place, record, leave open, forbid, attach, conceal, or photograph.
- Do not include vague tasks such as "think about mood" unless they are tied to a concrete object or note.
- Separate immediate preparations from future obligations when needed.
- If a material, card, token, or reference is chosen, name it clearly enough that Chronicle can recover the choice.
- If something remains unresolved, make a token, note, or log entry for it rather than relying on memory.
- The todo list should protect later phases from being overrun: Attunement may prepare a guide or token, but should not complete Surface or Inscription.

### Cartography

- Cartography changes the tile as a map-object: position, relation, role, inheritance, tether, layer, stack, or structural rule.
- For a new tile, read the result as "what kind of map-object is trying to appear" before deciding its visual surface.
- For an existing tile, read the result as an alteration of state or relation, not decoration.

### Surface

- Surface prepares the ground. It establishes body, texture, divisions, openings, and working conditions.
- Surface may make large physical moves, but it should leave meaningful space for Inscription.
- Surface answers "what can marks happen on, through, or against?"
- Surface can interpret drawn cards or omens in some amount, but should keep that interpretation more abstract than Inscription. Prefer translating cards into material behavior, pressure, atmosphere, opening, structure, or treatment rather than full imagery.
- When an old bleed debt reaches a newly created neighboring tile, Surface is usually the right phase to decide how it physically enters the new tile: clean continuation, misalignment, interruption, filtering, resistance, or transformation.

#### Surface Interpretation Rules

- Treat Surface as foundation, not final symbolism. It may change the tile physically, but it should not spend all meanings, names, routes, symbols, lore, or residues before Inscription.
- Read the six Surface categories as one physical procedure: Ground sets the base condition; Substance controls material choice; Application controls how much and where; Treatment controls finish or aging; Structure organizes the tile; Opening protects what remains unfinished.
- Always ask what earlier phases require Surface to answer. Attunement pressures, Cartography obligations, neighboring edges, unresolved wounds, tokens, reserves, and inherited omens should become working conditions on the surface.
- Translate Attunement into physical pressure. Echo may become absence, repetition, continuation, interruption, or a protected blank. Matter may select, forbid, reserve, or constrain materials. Mirror may influence shape, tone, route, tension, or contrast. Omen may become a wound, weather, sign-pressure, breach, stain, or opening. Pressure should steer density, division, openness, connection, or strangeness. Provision should become a token, reserve, tool, witness, fragment, or practical safeguard.
- Keep card interpretation partial and abstract. Cards may affect atmosphere, direction, rupture, passage, color behavior, surface damage, or central gravity, but should not usually become literal figures or complete scenes during Surface.
- If a result is vague, make it physical by choosing one action verb: wash, mask, tear, cut, stain, reserve, scrape, fold, divide, expose, cover, align, misalign, weather, press, layer, or leave blank.
- Preserve room for Inscription. Each Surface result should create at least one available place, boundary, route, mark-field, opening, residue, or question that later details can answer.
- When Surface creates a wound, breach, center, harbor, threshold, or reserve, record whether it is physically made now, merely implied, or held as a future obligation.
- Avoid over-completing the tile. If the Surface starts to explain exactly what everything is, stop and move that explanation into a future Inscription or Chronicle note.
- A good Surface instruction should tell the user what to do with hands and materials next: what to prepare, where to leave space, what to apply, what to avoid, and what must remain unresolved.

#### S003 / T002 Surface Example

- T002 confirmed that Bea bleeds north from T001 into T002 over T002's southern edge.
- Because T002 is Void-Born, Grid-Tangled, Bright, and Divider, Bea did not continue smoothly. It entered as misaligned pieces inside a 2 x 2 cm patch-grid.
- Plain Substance can be actual ordinary material: botanical motif paper, book page, art paper, thin cardboard, writing paper, and pencil grid.
- Interrupted Application can be answered by misaligned continuation, untouched gaps, skipped cells, and broken patch logic.
- Luminous Treatment can be a restrained signal such as a silver marker line, not a fully metallic or decorative surface.
- A Breach can be implied by a winding line, dry channel, seam, or broken continuity even before Inscription names it.

### Inscription

- Inscription adds specific marks, features, names, routes, symbols, residues, and lore-bearing details.
- It should answer earlier obligations when possible: Attunement pressures, Cartography rules, Surface openings, neighboring edges, and unresolved wounds.
- Inscription can resolve a pressure cleanly or deliberately leave residue for Chronicle.
- If a route or road enters but the Inscription Form is Mass, do not force it to remain a line. Let the route become area, field, stain, terrain, zone, or absorbed body.
- Force: Touching reaches an edge and creates a future debt, but it does not change a neighboring tile yet. Record the touched edge for Chronicle.

#### S003 / T002 Inscription Example

- T001's road enters T002 briefly from the southern edge.
- Because S003 Inscription is Local + Mass + Absorb + Source + Touching + Debt, the road should lose its route-quality and be swallowed by a contained patch-field mass.
- The source for the mass is not new imagery; it comes from T001, Bea, the road, the patch-grid, botanical/book/writing papers, yellow-orange wash, silver winding line, and card pressures.
- The mass may touch one edge of T002, but the next tile is not altered yet. Chronicle must record the edge and the debt.

### Chronicle

- Chronicle closes the session by making the work recoverable. It records, explains, archives, publishes, maintains, or seeds.
- Chronicle is where unclear rules and provisional rulings should be named as documentation debts.
- A good Chronicle note lets the next session understand what changed and what still asks.
