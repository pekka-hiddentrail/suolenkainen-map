# Existing-Tile Cartography Audit Note

Working note for Suolenkainen's Map.

Status: superseded by `map-creation-rules.md` Phase 2.2 Existing-Tile Cartography; retained as an audit note.

Context: S002 targets T001 at [0,0], an existing tile. At the time this note was written, the detailed procedure appeared unfinished. The main rules now include the existing-tile adjustment questions, six-number table, concrete effects, hard-bind handling, and result types, so this file is no longer an open rule debt.

## Core Ruling

An existing tile is not born again.

Its root behavior remains intact unless an explicit later rule changes root identity. Cartography for an existing tile should therefore adjust the tile's map-relations, obligations, orientation of attention, edge behavior, stack/layer state, or unresolved pressures.

For T001, the root behavior is fixed:

- Tile ID: T001.
- Coordinate: [0,0].
- Root identity: void-born harbor-route.
- Stable traits: origin tile, anchor point, four quadrants, route pressure, division, harbor function, omen inheritance, central structure, breach opening, local spreading pattern-system, and unresolved bleed debt.

S002 Cartography may not erase those traits. It may reveal which trait becomes active, strained, crowned, blocked, or made costly.

## Existing-Tile Cartography Questions

For an existing tile, replace the new-tile birth questions with adjustment questions:

1. Root: What existing behavior must remain unchanged?
2. Activation: Which old trait wakes up now?
3. Adjustment: What physical or relational change happens to the tile as a map-object?
4. Constraint: What cannot be changed, covered, moved, or forgotten?
5. Cost: What new debt, marker, layer, edge condition, or future rule is created?
6. Carry-forward: What rule, state, or constraint must later phases inherit?

These questions keep the phase cartographic rather than decorative.

## Historical Existing-Tile Result Types

These result types were originally provisional. They have since been folded into the stable existing-tile Cartography rules.

| Result Type | Behavior | Physical / Recordable Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Re-aim | The tile stays in place, but attention turns toward one edge, route, wound, quadrant, or center. | Mark the active direction in the log; optionally place a temporary arrow, note, or token beside the tile. |
| Re-bind | One unresolved obligation becomes the tile's active bond. | Name the bond: edge debt, wound debt, archive-name, fragment, neighborless bleed, or center anchor. |
| Rotate attention | The tile does not physically rotate, but the session reads it from a new side or orientation. | Record the working orientation for this session. Do not redraw north unless a later rule says so. |
| Expose | A buried or unfinished earlier condition becomes active again. | Bring forward an older note, photo, fragment, card, label, or unresolved mark. |
| Layer | Add or reserve a layer relation without changing the base identity. | Create a removable overlay, attached fragment, transparent sheet, label, envelope, or stack note. |
| Tighten | A loose debt becomes more specific. | Choose exact edge, exact wound, exact fragment, exact name, or exact condition. |
| Complicate | Two existing traits interfere with each other. | Record the contradiction and require Surface/Inscription to answer it. |
| Suspend | A pressure is acknowledged but not acted on yet. | Create a Chronicle debt explaining why the pressure was withheld. |

## Best-Guess Ruling For S002 / T001

S002 Cartography should be an adjustment, not a new birth.

Recommended result:

T001 remains the void-born harbor-route, but its unresolved bleed debt is re-bound through the edge-vs-center conflict. The tile is not moved, replaced, retired, or reborn. Instead, Cartography activates the question of whether the map is governed by the center anchor or by the edge where the wound or patch-field wants to escape.

The active adjustment is:

- Root preserved: T001 remains the origin and void-born harbor-route.
- Activation: unresolved bleed edge wakes up.
- Adjustment: the tile is re-aimed from the center toward the unchosen bleed edge.
- Constraint: the central wallpaper diamond / anchor point must remain authoritative and visible.
- Cost: the session must either choose the official bleed edge or create a stronger reason for delaying that choice.
- Carry-forward: Surface and Inscription must make the edge-vs-center conflict physically legible.

## Relation To S002 Attunement

S002 Attunement created six binding pressures:

- Echo: an unresolved previous-session note influences T001.
- Matter: one material family must be chosen.
- Mirror: alien boundary, hybridized with another incompatible reference.
- Omen: archive-name crowned as dominant.
- Pressure: edge-vs-center conflict.
- Provision: attached fragment becomes a hard obligation.

Existing-tile Cartography should translate these into map-object behavior:

- Echo becomes the active old bond: the bleed edge or mystery wound.
- Matter becomes the permitted family for cartographic adjustment, probably paper / printed matter.
- Mirror becomes an alien boundary logic imposed on T001's existing edge, wound, quadrant division, or harbor border.
- Omen becomes a crowned archive-name that governs the adjustment.
- Pressure becomes the main cartographic conflict: edge vs center.
- Provision becomes a removable or attached fragment that must be tracked as part of the tile's state.

## Physical Cartography Instruction

Do not yet paint, decorate, or complete symbolic marks.

Do this instead:

1. Place T001 in its normal orientation.
2. Identify the central anchor / wallpaper diamond as the fixed authority.
3. Choose one candidate bleed edge, or place a temporary marker on the strongest candidate edge.
4. Place the bound fragment near or lightly attached to that edge, center, or threshold, without permanently resolving it unless the later phase demands it.
5. Write the crowned archive-name on a temporary label, not necessarily on the tile yet.
6. Record the working orientation: "S002 reads T001 from center toward the candidate bleed edge."
7. Record whether the candidate edge is chosen officially or only nominated.

## What Must Not Change

- Do not move T001 away from [0,0].
- Do not erase the origin identity.
- Do not replace the void-born harbor-route behavior.
- Do not treat this as a new tile.
- Do not resolve the bound fragment silently.
- Do not bury the central anchor beyond recognition.
- Do not forget the previous bleed debt; either specify it or preserve it as a named delay.
